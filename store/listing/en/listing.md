```
MuZhao — a photo studio in your pocket.

Straight talk, up front:

• Fully offline — everything runs on your device. Photos never leave your phone. The app requests no network permission at all.
• Completely free — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads.
• No watermarks — clean results, ready for applications and official filings.

[7 PRINT-GRADE SIZES]
1-inch 25×35mm | small 1-inch 22×32mm | large 1-inch 33×48mm |
2-inch 35×49mm | small 2-inch 35×45mm | social security 26×32mm |
US visa 51×51mm
All exported at 300 DPI with exact pixel dimensions, ready to print.

[6 BACKGROUND COLORS, ONE TAP]
White, blue, red, dark blue, light grey, and blue gradient.
Six candidates side by side — just pick the one you like.

[HOW IT WORKS]
1. Pick a front-facing photo from your gallery (JPG / PNG supported)
2. Drag the corners to fine-tune the crop — the aspect ratio stays locked
3. Wait a moment while the on-device model performs the portrait cutout
4. Choose a background and press the brass button to save to your gallery

[ABOUT PRIVACY]
This app requests no INTERNET permission, and contains no analytics SDK and
no advertising SDK. Portrait cutout runs entirely on your device — your
photos stay on your phone from start to finish.

Wood, brass and felt — may it serve you like an old, reliable camera.
```
