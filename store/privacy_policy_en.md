# MuZhao Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

MuZhao is an ID photo tool that runs entirely offline. This policy rests on three commitments, stated plainly:

## 1. We collect nothing

The app **does not collect any personal information**. We do not collect your name, contact details, location, device identifiers — and we **do not collect, read, or access any photo** you take, select, or generate.

The app contains no analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting components. As a result, we have no way of knowing how you use the app.

## 2. We transmit nothing

The app **does not send any data to any server**. From the moment a photo enters the app, every step — portrait cutout, cropping, background replacement, encoding, saving — happens locally on your device.

**The app does not request the INTERNET permission.** At a technical level it is incapable of sending your photos anywhere. You can verify this on the app's system permission page.

## 3. Local storage only

- Photos you select remain where they are in your system gallery; the app does not copy them elsewhere;
- A finished photo is written to your device gallery only after you explicitly tap "Save";
- Uninstalling the app removes nothing from your gallery and leaves no residual app data directory behind.

## Other

- The app contains **no ads**, no third-party SDKs, and no payment or in-app purchase functionality;
- The app is suitable for all ages and contains no age-restricted content;
- If a future version ever changes any of the above, it will be stated plainly in the release notes, and the "collect nothing, transmit nothing" commitments will not change.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach us through the developer contact provided on the store listing page.
